## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-11

### Changed
- The six lodgers are redrawn: clearer faces and shapes, a striped cat, a moth in round glasses, a bullfinch in profile, a ringed tail for the raccoon.
- The interface is evening blue instead of dark brown: dusk indigo, cream paper, brass and lingonberry red.
- Smoother animation: lodgers breathe slowly instead of twitching, and the stove, the jars and the hearts move at twice the frame rate.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-30

### Added
- A pixel room with a pantry of glowing jars, a tiled stove, a window with the real time of day and the real weather of your town.
- Catching cosiness four ways: ten seconds of sound, the light of a photo taken with the phone's own camera, three minutes of quiet (only the loudness is measured), and walks from the step counter, by hand or on their own.
- One tap for what it was (weather, walk, tea and food, warmth, something new, people, by hand); every jar gets a handwritten label, the date, the town and the weather.
- Six lodgers who each eat one kind: the cat, the dormouse, the moth in glasses, the bullfinch, the hedgehog and the raccoon. The cat and the dormouse live here from the start; the others come with the first jar of their kind.
- Nobody dies: hungry lodgers sleep pale in knitted scarves, full ones glow, and in the evening they pile up by the stove.
- Dreams as riddles; a dream that comes true leaves a gift in the room.
- Opening a jar plays what it caught (jars without a recording sound like their kind), the stove flares and everyone gathers to listen.
- Jam: two jars cooked into one that feeds both lodgers.
- Rare jars in snow and thunderstorms, and a "First snow" jar once a season.
- English and Russian.
